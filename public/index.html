How to play JUST clONE
  1. Each round, one player is the guesser. Everyone else sees a secret word.
  2. Everyone else secretly writes one word as a clue — no repeats of the secret word, and no numbers or gestures.
  3. Any clues that match each other exactly are automatically removed before the guesser sees them.
  4. The guesser sees the remaining clues and gets one guess at the secret word.
  5. It's fully cooperative — your whole group shares one score, out of the number of rounds played.
